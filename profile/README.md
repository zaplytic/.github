# Welcome to Zaplytic! 👋

We're an independent software studio in Bangladesh — a tight team of four across engineering, product, design and relationships. We build robust SaaS products, custom websites, mobile apps and business software, plus open-source tools for developers.

Everything we ship starts from a real problem we've hit firsthand, not a trend we're chasing. If we wouldn't use it ourselves, it doesn't ship.

## What We Stand For

*   **Robust software** — We invest in the boring parts: error handling, tests and observability, so what we build keeps working under real load.
*   **Keep our promises** — Deadlines, scopes and support commitments mean something. We'd rather renegotiate upfront than quietly miss.
*   **Empower everyone** — The same tool should respect a hobbyist's weekend project and a team's production stack.
*   **Earn trust, not lock-in** — Your data, your exports, your exit. No proprietary formats, no hostage data.
*   **Default to simple** — We'd rather remove a feature than bury it behind a setting.

## What We Do

*   **SaaS Products** — [ZapRoute](https://zaproute.com.bd), a travel agency ERP that keeps things simple, and ZapMemo, an offline-first digital memo and receipt system for small shops.
*   **Open-Source Tools** — [RailGuard](https://github.com/zaplytic/railguard), self-hosted crash reporting, performance and error monitoring.
*   **Client Work** — Custom websites, Android and iOS apps, ERP/POS systems and e-commerce stores for businesses across Bangladesh, with ongoing support after launch.

## How We Build

Ruby on Rails is our go-to stack. It powers ZapRoute, ZapMemo and most of our client work, and lets a small team ship fast without cutting corners.

Alongside Rails, we build with Elixir and Phoenix. The BEAM gives us fault tolerance and concurrency out of the box, so our software keeps running when things go wrong. Phoenix LiveView lets us ship real-time, interactive interfaces from the server, with less JavaScript to maintain. We test it all with ExUnit.
